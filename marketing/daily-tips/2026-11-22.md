# Jetleaf Daily Tip — Sunday (2026-11-22)

## Slide Breakdown

- **Slide 1: Title/Hook**
  - **Text:** Stop Writing Boilerplate Error Handling.
  - **Subtext:** Master Declarative Resilience in Dart with Jetleaf.
  - **Visual:** Jetleaf Logo centered. Large, bold typography.

- **Slide 2: The Problem**
  - **Text:** The "Try-Catch" Trap.
  - **Content:** Are your services buried in nested `try-catch` blocks and manual retry loops? It’s brittle, hard to test, and kills your business logic readability. Real enterprise systems need declarative fault tolerance.

- **Slide 3: The Jetleaf Way**
  - **Text:** Resilience via Annotations.
  - **Code Snippet:**
    ```dart
    @Service()
    class PaymentService {
      @Retryable(
        attempts: 3,
        delay: Duration(milliseconds: 500),
        on: [NetworkException]
      )
      Future<void> process(Payment p) async {
        // Business logic stays clean
      }
    }
    ```

- **Slide 4: Under the Hood**
  - **Text:** How `@Retryable` works.
  - **Content:** Jetleaf uses proxy-based interception. When the `JetleafVM` detects the `@Retryable` annotation, it wraps your method in a specialized `RecoveryInterceptor`. It manages state, backoff calculation, and Isolate-safe exception propagation automatically.

- **Slide 5: Pro-Tip**
  - **Text:** Combine with `@Transactional`.
  - **Content:** When combined with `jetleaf_data`, `@Transactional` ensures that if a retryable operation eventually fails after all attempts, your database state is rolled back automatically. Keep your integrity, keep your sanity.
