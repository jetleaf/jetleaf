- **Slide 4: Deep Dive**
  - **Heading:** How it works under the hood.
  - **Text:** The `JetleafVM` resident daemon maintains a long-lived Isolate pool. Unlike standard `dart run`, we perform AST-level incremental scanning. Only the modified `Pod` or `Controller` is re-linked into the IoC container, bypassing the expensive bootstrap phase.

- **Slide 5: Pro-Tip**
  - **Heading:** Optimize Your Dev Cycle.
  - **Text:** Use `jl test --watch` alongside `jl dev`. Because Jetleaf shares the same IoC container between your app and your test suite, you can run integration tests against your running dev server without spinning up a separate test database.

## Ready-to-Copy Social Caption

Stop "context-switching" while waiting for your backend to compile. 🛑☕️

If you’re a Flutter developer building your own backend, you know the pain: you save your file, switch to the terminal, wait for the VM to start, switch back, and lose your flow. 

Today is **#JetleafVelocity** day. ⚡️

With the `JetleafVM` resident daemon, we’ve brought the Flutter "Hot Reload" experience to the server side. By keeping your IoC container warm and pre-warmed, `jl dev` gives you sub-100ms iteration times. 

No more waiting. Just pure, type-safe development from database to UI.
